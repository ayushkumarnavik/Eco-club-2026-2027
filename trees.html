🌿 Neem Tree (Azadirachta indica)

Neem is one of the most valuable medicinal trees of India. It possesses antibacterial, antiviral and antifungal properties. Every part of the tree, including leaves, bark and seeds, is useful. Neem helps purify air, improves soil quality and provides shade.

Benefits

  • ✓ Natural medicine
  • ✓ Purifies air
  • ✓ Eco-friendly pesticide
  • ✓ Controls harmful insects

🌳 Banyan Tree (Ficus benghalensis)

The Banyan Tree is the national tree of India and is famous for its huge canopy and aerial roots. It provides shelter to birds and animals and has a long lifespan.

Benefits

  • ✓ Provides shade
  • ✓ Maintains biodiversity
  • ✓ Prevents soil erosion
  • ✓ Cultural importance

🍃 Peepal Tree (Ficus religiosa)

Peepal is regarded as a sacred tree in India. It is known for its ability to absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen efficiently. It supports many species of birds and insects.

Benefits

  • ✓ Helps in air purification
  • ✓ Supports biodiversity
  • ✓ Medicinal importance
  • ✓ Environmental balance

🥭 Mango Tree (Mangifera indica)

The Mango Tree is famous for producing delicious fruits and providing dense shade. It contributes to ecological balance and offers habitat to many organisms.

Benefits

  • ✓ Nutritious fruits
  • ✓ Provides oxygen
  • ✓ Supports birds and insects
  • ✓ Gives shade

🌲 Ashoka Tree (Saraca asoca)

Ashoka is an ornamental and medicinal tree commonly planted in gardens and along roads. It is valued for its beautiful foliage and environmental significance.

Benefits

  • ✓ Enhances beauty
  • ✓ Medicinal properties
  • ✓ Air purification
  • ✓ Helps reduce pollution

🌱 Tulsi Plant (Ocimum tenuiflorum)

Tulsi, also known as Holy Basil, is considered a sacred plant and is widely used in traditional medicine. It helps purify air and possesses antibacterial properties.

Benefits

  • ✓ Improves immunity
  • ✓ Medicinal importance
  • ✓ Purifies surroundings
  • ✓ Rich in antioxidants

🌴 Coconut Tree (Cocos nucifera)

The Coconut Tree is often called the "Tree of Life" because almost every part of the tree is useful. It provides food, oil, fiber and shelter.

Benefits

  • ✓ Provides nutritious food
  • ✓ Produces coconut oil
  • ✓ Eco-friendly products
  • ✓ Supports livelihoods

🌍 Importance of Trees

Trees are essential for life on Earth. They produce oxygen, absorb carbon dioxide, prevent soil erosion, regulate climate, support biodiversity and provide food, medicines and shelter. Planting and protecting trees is one of the most effective ways to combat climate change and maintain ecological balance.
