🌿 ECO CLUB OF DAV PUBLIC SCHOOL NIT CAMPUS ADITYAPUR 🌿

Detailed Information & School Highlights

🌍 Why is the Eco Club of DAV Public School, NIT Campus, Jamshedpur Popular?

The Eco Club of DAV Public School, NIT Campus, Adityapur, is recognized for its active participation in environmental awareness programs, campus sustainability initiatives, and community outreach activities. The club promotes environmental consciousness among students through various educational and practical activities that help create responsible citizens.

🌳 Plantation Drives

The Eco Club regularly conducts tree plantation and greenery campaigns. Students actively participate in planting saplings and learning the importance of preserving biodiversity and maintaining ecological balance. Such activities encourage environmental responsibility and promote a greener future.

🏙 City-Level Participation

Students of the school have participated in various environmental awareness events, exhibitions, and competitions organized by educational and social organizations. These activities provide opportunities to showcase innovative ideas related to waste management, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable development.

♻ Campus Hygiene and Green Practices

The Eco Club promotes cleanliness drives, waste segregation, recycling activities, and responsible use of resources. Students are encouraged to reduce plastic use and adopt eco-friendly practices that contribute to maintaining a clean and healthy environment.

📢 Community Awareness Programs

The club extends its awareness activities beyond the school campus. Members encourage friends, families, and society to adopt environmentally friendly habits and contribute towards building a cleaner and greener community.
