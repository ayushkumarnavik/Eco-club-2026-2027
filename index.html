What is Eco Club ?

An Eco Club is a student-based organization formed to promote awareness about environmental protection, conservation of natural resources, and sustainable living. It encourages students to develop habits that help preserve nature and motivates them to actively participate in activities such as plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns, recycling programs, water conservation, and spreading awareness among society. Eco Clubs help create responsible citizens who understand the importance of maintaining ecological balance.

What is Eco Club of DAV NIT?

The Eco Club of D.A.V Public School, NIT Campus, Adityapur is a student-led environmental initiative working under the guidance of teachers and school authorities. The club aims to encourage environmental consciousness among students through various awareness campaigns, plantation activities, waste management practices, and participation in environmental programs. It seeks to develop a culture of responsibility towards nature and sustainable development among students.

What is the Function of Eco Club?

The major function of an Eco Club is to develop environmental awareness and practical understanding among students. It organizes tree plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns, recycling projects, water conservation activities, poster competitions, rallies, seminars, and awareness programs. Through these activities, students learn the importance of protecting biodiversity and maintaining a healthy environment.

What is the Need of Eco Club in DAV NIT?

In today's world, environmental issues such as pollution, climate change, deforestation, and excessive use of resources are increasing rapidly. Therefore, there is a need to educate young minds regarding environmental responsibilities. Eco Club in DAV NIT provides a platform where students can understand environmental challenges and contribute positively towards creating a cleaner, greener, and healthier society. It also develops leadership qualities and teamwork among students.
